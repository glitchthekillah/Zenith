# 0.2.7

**Linux**
- Video now plays inside the app window. No more separate player window, and no flickering when you move the mouse over the video.
- Your server repackages the file into a stream on the fly. The video itself is copied as is, never re-encoded, so quality is the same as the original.
- Audio tracks play as they are (AAC, AC3, EAC3, Opus, FLAC). DTS and TrueHD are converted to AAC.
- Text subtitles work. Picture subtitles (PGS) aren't shown on Linux yet and are greyed out in the menu.

**Everyone**
- New pause look: a frosted glass "Paused" pill that matches the rest of the app. Click anywhere to pause or resume and double-click for fullscreen, same as before.
- The seek preview, on-screen messages and track menu use the same glass style.
- If a show defaults to picture subtitles (PGS) that the app can't show, it now plays with subtitles off instead of stopping on an error.
- Works with Jellyfin 12.2, which stopped accepting the old way the app sent its login token for images, video and subtitles.
