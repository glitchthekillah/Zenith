# 0.2.8

**Home**
- New banner: the picture fills the whole banner, sharper than before, with the movie's logo art when your server has it. Hover over it to pause, use the arrows (or arrow keys, or swipe) to move between titles, and watch the bar fill until the next one.
- Add anything to My list straight from the banner with the heart button.
- New "My list" row with everything you've hearted, newest first.
- Your libraries got a fresh look: each tile shows what's inside with a backdrop and a fan of posters, sorted so Movies, Shows and Collections sit together.
- The greeting now shows a proper "Continue watching" card with a picture, progress and time left.
- Hover a Continue watching card and press the X to remove it from the row.
- Titles on 3 or more servers now say "On 3 servers" instead of listing every name.

**Player**
- Sleep timer: stop after this episode, or pause in 15, 30 or 60 minutes. Find it in the player settings.
- "Still watching?" check after 3 episodes play back to back without you touching anything.
