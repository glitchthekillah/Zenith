# 0.2.15

- Search shows results from your servers as you type, with poster, year, type, rating, whether you've watched it and which servers have it. Arrow keys to move, Enter to open, or press Enter straight away for all results.
- When a video won't play on your device, Zenith now asks the server to convert it and keeps playing from the same spot.
- Converted audio keeps surround sound (up to 5.1) instead of being squashed to stereo.
- The player's info panel says exactly what the server is doing: direct play, direct stream, or transcoding audio or video.
