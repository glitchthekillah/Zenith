# 0.2.11

**Faster on Linux**
- Much quicker and smoother on Linux, especially scrolling and opening pages: heavy blur effects are turned off there and a slow graphics mode is avoided.
- Videos that wouldn't start on Linux now play, and if a stream really can't play you get a clear reason instead of endless loading.

**Playback**
- Files with audio your computer can't play (like EAC3, DTS or TrueHD) now play: the server converts just the audio, the picture stays untouched.
- Choose how far the skip buttons jump (1 to 60 seconds, 10 by default) in Settings, Playback. The buttons, arrow keys and media keys all follow it.
- "Ends at" time next to the clock in the player and on movie and episode pages.
- Scroll over the volume control to change the volume.
- Press ? in the player to see every keyboard shortcut.
- A proper screen while a video plays in picture-in-picture, with a button to bring it back.

**Home and shows**
- Continue watching shows one card per show: the episode you watched last (go back to rewatch and it follows you).
- Pages load calmly: placeholders hold the layout, rows slide in and pictures fade in.
- Rows dissolve at the edges instead of cutting cards off, and the arrows only appear when there's more to see.
- Bolder row titles and page titles.
- Season tabs show how much you've watched, plus "Mark everything before this as watched" and "Random episode".
- Undo after marking something watched or removing it from Continue watching.
- Cast and crew can be scrolled with arrows; long names stay tidy.
- Darker episode rows that are easier to read.
- Recent searches under the search box, and a back-to-top button on long pages.
- Our own tooltips instead of the system ones.

**Requests**
- Much more detail on request pages: ratings, trailer, cast, director and writers, release info, studio, budget and box office.
- If a title is already on one of your servers, a "Watch on …" button takes you straight there.
- With more than one request server, pick which one to request on; each shows its own quality options.

**Fixes**
- Book and comic pages without a banner no longer show a dark slanted edge behind the covers.
- Detail page backgrounds fade in under the toolbar instead of being cut off.
