# 0.2.18

- New: style presets in Settings > Appearance > Style. Each one changes the whole look of the app, not just the colours:
  - **Marathon**: acid graphics, hard edges, specimen frames and glitch motion, with its own accent colours and optional interface sounds.
  - **Phantom**: red, black and white, slanted panels and cut-paper type.
  - **Codec**: a green phosphor terminal with scanlines and glowing text.
  - **Atelier**: an editorial film journal with serif type and hairlines.
- Every style has its own light and dark mode.
- The server loading indicator is cleaner and follows the current style.
