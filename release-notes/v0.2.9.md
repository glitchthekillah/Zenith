# 0.2.9

- Cleaner greeting at the top of Home: the faded picture behind it is gone, so the card stays crisp and easy to read.
- New server loading indicator: a small glass card slides up at the bottom of the screen while your servers load, says which ones (for example "Loading Andromeda"), and slides away when they're done. It no longer covers the top of the page.
- Better banner pictures: the whole picture now shows on the right at its proper shape instead of being zoomed and cropped, and it fades smoothly into the title side.
- The slide arrows moved into a small control in the corner next to the timer bars, so they no longer sit on top of the text.
