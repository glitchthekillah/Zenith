# 0.2.14

- New backgrounds in Settings > Appearance: Aurora, Nebula, Silk, Embers, Contours and Rays, plus Showcase (cycles through what's on your servers), your own picture, a title you search for, and a solid colour you pick. Liquid stays the default.
- Background cards show live previews, and switching backgrounds fades smoothly.
- The background leans toward the mouse much more subtly.
- Changing the background palette no longer resets your highlight and button colours.
- Linux: the background shows right away on startup, and stray marks around titles in rows are gone.
