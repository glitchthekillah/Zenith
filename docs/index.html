Your servers.
One beautiful app.

Zenith brings your Jellyfin movies and shows, your Kavita books and manga, and your Seerr requests together in one desktop app. It talks only to the servers you sign in to.

01

Home that knows you

A spotlight that rotates through your library, everything you were halfway through, what's next in each show and what just landed. Open the app and you're one click from playing.

02

Every show, laid out

Seasons, episodes and where you stopped. Resume, play a random episode, mark a season watched, watch the trailer.

03

A player that just plays

Plays the original file when it can and asks your server to convert it when it can't. Subtitles, audio tracks, quality, speed, chapters, skip intro and picture in picture.

04

Search everything

Start typing and results show up across every server you've connected. Arrow keys to move, Enter to open.

05

Libraries with real filters

Not watched, in progress, favourites, genres, sorting and shuffle. Find tonight's movie fast.

06

Yours, and your friends'

Each card is one server. Add your own Jellyfin, Kavita and request site, then a friend's, and everything shows up together, marked with where it's from.
